Introducing the Build Time Evaluation Experience for watsonx Orchestrate

We're excited to announce the release of the new Build Time Evaluation Experience in watsonx Orchestrate! Directly within the UI, builders can now record conversations and simulate them with their agent to rapidly test performance at scale. All before releasing to end users!

This powerful new experience enables you to simulate hundreds of conversations at once, giving you fast, comprehensive insights into how your agent behaves in the real-world scenarios. Whether you're iterating on instructions, refining tool descriptions, or tuning your agent's behavior, this feature puts robust testing capabilities right at your fingertips.

Key Capabilities

The Build Time Evaluation Experience is designed to give AI builders confidence in their agents before they ever reach an end user. Here's what it enables:

  • Build-time agent testing: Validate your agent's behavior during the build phase, catching issues before they impact users.
  • Simulation at scale: Simulate hundreds of conversations simultaneously, dramatically reducing the time required for manual testing.
  • Journey success testing: Define and assess success criteria such as expected tool calls with desired parameters and final response quality, giving you a clear picture of how well your agent completes its intended tasks.
  • Automated test case generation: Key details are automatically extracted from saved conversations and converted into structured test cases, minimizing manual effort.
  • Performance benchmarking: Establish a baseline success rate for your agent and track improvements over time as you make updates, without spending hours running tests manually.

How It Works

Getting started with the Build Time Evaluation Experience is straightforward. Here's how to go from a conversation to a full evaluation in just a few steps:

  1. Have a conversation with your agent. Use the chat interface to interact with the agent you're building, just as an end user would.
  2. Save the conversation. Choose to save the conversation directly from the chat interface.
  3. Review extracted details. Key context details are automatically pulled from the conversation and converted into test cases. You'll have the opportunity to review and adjust what was extracted before saving.
  4. Manage your test cases. Navigate to the test experience to review the full set of test cases you've created across your sessions.
  5. Run your evaluation. Execute the tests to receive evaluation results, including an overall journey success score across all specified test cases.
  6. Iterate and improve. Use your evaluation results as a benchmark. As you adjust agent instructions, update tool descriptions, or modify the tools themselves, re-run your tests to quickly see how your agent's success rate improves over time, no more hours spent on manual testing.

Learn more by checking out the Evaluate with saved conversations documentation.

What's Next

This release is just the beginning. We plan to continue building on this experience with a range of enhancements, including:

  • Greater debugging capabilities built directly into failure cases, so you can quickly understand why a test failed and take targeted action to fix it.
  • Improved comparison experiences to make it easier to evaluate results across different versions of your agent side by side.
  • Optimization capabilities to help you not just identify issues, but actively improve your agent's performance based on evaluation insights.

Stay tuned! There's a lot more to come.

We're thrilled to put this capability into the hands of builders and can't wait to see what you create.

Thank you for being part of the watsonx Orchestrate community. Happy building!

Questions or thoughts? Try out the Build Time Evaluation Experience in watsonx Orchestrate today and let us know what you think. Your feedback is invaluable as we continue to shape and improve this experience.
